# Landfall

Inferred from docs/DESIGN.md and the owner's brief of 2026-11-20 (subagent, no interview channel; assumptions labeled).

## Product
Landfall makes a small business ready for personal AI agents: it reads the site (static scan with receipts), has a
browser agent try real customer tasks, asks an agent whether the business is found, writes a fix package, applies it
to a mirror and re-checks. Output: a plain-English owner report.

## Users
- Small business owners outside tech: need "what is broken, what do I do" in plain words.
- Hackathon judges (owner answer 2026-11-20): the report is shown on a projector; a 5-second read from the back of
  the room, one wow moment, then receipts.

## Brand
Owner-approved world: Harbor / Shipyard (carved varnished quarterboards, gilt heavy Clarendon, manila cargo tags on
twine, yellow enamel plate, slate tally). Landfall = where ships come ashore. Fun but crafted and well kept, never
distressed. No realistic people, no copyrighted characters, no em dashes.

## Truth constraints
Every finding carries a receipt. Host-level blocks are labeled as a host setting, never as fixed. Read-only on real sites.
